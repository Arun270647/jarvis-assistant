Jarvis

A free, open-source voice assistant for your PC. It hears and speaks on your computer, and works with your own Claude account or another AI.

Install it in three steps

  1. Open your Downloads folder, right-click the zip file and choose Extract All, then Extract.
  2. Open the extracted Jarvis folder and double-click Jarvis.exe.
  3. The setup asks for a name, downloads the models and connects your AI. At the end, Install for me moves Jarvis out of Downloads and adds it to the Start Menu.

"Windows protected your PC"

Jarvis is free and has no budget for a code-signing certificate, so Windows SmartScreen warns the first time you open it. Click More info, then Run anyway. Windows only asks once.

To make sure the file is the one published here, open PowerShell in your Downloads folder and run:

Get-FileHash Jarvis-windows-x64.zip
